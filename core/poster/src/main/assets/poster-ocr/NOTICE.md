# Bundled OCR models

English (`eng`), Kannada (`kan`), Hindi (`hin`): official tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast,
revision 87416418657359cb625c412a48b6e1d6d41c29bd.
https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast/tree/87416418657359cb625c412a48b6e1d6d41c29bd

Models are fast integer LSTM data, licensed Apache-2.0; see LICENSE in this folder.
Runtime: Tesseract4Android 4.9.0 (Apache-2.0), including Tesseract and Leptonica
and their bundled native-library notices. Models are copied only to app-private
storage on first use. There is no outbound image/text upload or per-scan charge.

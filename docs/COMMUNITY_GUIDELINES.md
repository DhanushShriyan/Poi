# Poi community guidelines

Poi helps people find and safely enjoy real-world events.

## Required

- Publish accurate dates, times, locations, prices, and organizer details.
- Have permission before listing a private event or another organization’s event.
- Respect event capacity, venue rules, intellectual property, and photo consent.
- Protect the identity and location of people who have not chosen to share them.
- Correct or cancel a listing promptly when details change.

## Prohibited

- Fraudulent events, fake tickets, impersonation, or misleading promotions.
- Harassment, threats, hate, sexual exploitation, or encouragement of violence.
- Publishing private addresses, live locations, phone numbers, or other personal information without consent.
- Photographs of people—especially minors—in a private setting without appropriate consent.
- Illegal goods, unsafe challenges, malware, spam, or coordinated manipulation of attendance and verification.

Poi may reduce distribution, remove content, restrict an account, preserve evidence, or refer urgent matters to appropriate authorities. Reporting must be available on all user-generated event and media surfaces.
